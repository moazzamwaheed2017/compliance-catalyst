## 3. Platform Architecture & Pillars

### **Pillar 1: Automatic AI Footprint Discovery**

Discover models, inference pipelines, and shadow AI deployed across your cloud accounts, SSO, and code repos via native connector integrations.

- Zero client agent overhead.

- Strictly inventories client organization assets—never platform infrastructure.

- Identifies unowned/orphan models and triggers decision SLAs.

### **Pillar 2: Connectors & Evidence Harvester**

The pipeline data plane that routes raw payloads into normalized compliance evidence.

- **Dual-Path Ingestion**: Routes structured API/JSON payloads and visual document captures (screenshots, logs, reports).

- **Visible Model Attribution**: Track handoffs end-to-end between the *Evidence Harvester* and downstream compliance engines.

### **Pillar 3: Dual-Pass Controls & Evidence Hub**

Eliminate AI mapping hallucinations with independent model verification.

- **Pass 1 (Control Mapping)**: Proposes evidence-to-clause mappings and initial confidence scores.

- **Pass 2 (Verification Pass)**: Cross-checks primary mappings to flag discrepancies before high-confidence baseline approval.

### **Pillar 4: Gated Remediation Kanban**

Fast-friction task execution board powered by the *Remediation Orchestrator Model*.

- **Gated Autonomy**: Remediation tasks auto-draft upon Risk Approval (7.6)—never acting without human sign-off.

- **1-Click Inline Acceptance**: Pre-filled owner and due-date suggestions from historical mitigation playbooks.

- **Automatic Residual Risk Recalculation**: Task completion automatically lowers residual risk scores across live telemetry.

## 4. Why Gated Autonomy?

> *"Full AI autonomy in compliance violates European AI Act Article 14 human oversight mandates. Manual GRC creates unacceptable engineering bottlenecks. ReguLattice engineered **Gated Autonomy**—background models draft, verify, and route evidence in real time, but execution requires human authorization."*
